Key Findings

  • Production experience is the #1 blocker. Every not-qualified job failed because of "no production experience" in the specific domain (claims processing, insurance verification, billing platforms, coding). RCM coursework alone is not bridging the gap.
  • Qualified jobs share a pattern: They require coursework-level knowledge (HIPAA, RCM, data analysis) rather than production experience. Dane Street (bill review), LHH (RCM billing), Stanford (authorization) all accepted coursework as transferable.
  • Salary range: Qualified jobs range from $30-42/hr. Not-qualified jobs include both higher ($60-80K Modivcare) and lower ($18-22/hr Integra Connect) — salary is not the differentiator, domain experience is.
  • Remote + Easy Apply jobs have higher applicant counts (77-100+) but also higher qualification rates when the role matches coursework-level requirements.

Recommendations (Preliminary)

  • Target roles that accept coursework-level knowledge: Bill review, RCM billing analyst, authorization coordinator, claims processing (entry-level), patient access/member services.
  • Avoid roles requiring production experience or certifications you don't have: Coding auditor (requires CPC/CCA/CCS), claims examiner (requires 5+ years), insurance agent (requires license).
  • Consider entry-level patient access/member services roles as a side door — lower barrier, healthcare domain, can build production experience.
  • Complete Medical Billing Specialist Certificate (Feb 1, 2027) and pass CPB exam — this will unlock higher-tier roles.
  • Consider volunteer or contract work in billing/claims to build production experience that coursework alone cannot provide.
